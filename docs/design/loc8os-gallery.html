Loc8OS · Appliance image · design reference

What the box looks like when it's alive

The Gateway is headless — Loc8OS has no screen of its own. It is seen on four surfaces: the status lens on the enclosure, the bench serial console, the claim & health cards inside Loc8 Command, and the HQ portfolio tile. Everything below is a design mock — the spec is docs/software/loc8os.md; none of these tools exist yet.

01 · The status lens — the entire on-device UI

One light, printed on the quick-start card. State is encoded in the waveform (breath, pulse, chase), never hue alone — red/amber is the classic colour-vision confusion pair.

UNCLAIMEDwhite chase — fresh from factory, waiting to be claimed
CLAIMED-IDLEgreen slow breath — mesh up, no shift running
LIVEgreen breath — shift live, consoles attached, synced
LIVE-NOSYNCamber pulse — uplink down, store-and-forward
ON-BATTERYamber breath, quickening as reserve drains
FAULTsolid red — a service failed and did not recover
UPDATINGwhite pulse — A/B slot write. Never on battery
