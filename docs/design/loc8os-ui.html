The OS itself

There is no desktop — Loc8OS is an appliance image and hides its Linux on purpose. But the OS does show its own face at three moments: boot (serial, factory/bench only), the local service portal any installer can reach from a phone browser, and recovery mode. All mocks — spec §7a in docs/software/loc8os.md.

Only ever seen at the factory or on a bench. In the field the box boots silently; the lens does the talking.

03 · Recovery mode
